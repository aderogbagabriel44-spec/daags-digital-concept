Frequently asked questions

Questions people may have before they contact us.

These answers can be edited as the business grows and official service policies are confirmed.

What services does Daags Digital Concept offer?

We currently focus on CAC registration, NIN services, fintech services, website design, graphic design, proof of address services and other online services.

Can you build a website for my business?

Yes. We can discuss the type of business, goals, pages, content, functionality and visual direction needed for the website.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. Share the existing website and explain what you want improved. We can review the structure, design and user experience before proposing the next step.

Do I need to have all my website content ready?

Not necessarily. We can help structure the information you already have and identify what still needs to be supplied before launch.

How do I request a service?

Use the contact page, call the listed number or send a WhatsApp message. Tell us the service you need and we will guide you on the next step.

Are all services available at all times?

Availability and requirements can change. Please confirm the current service details with Daags Digital Concept before making payment or starting a transaction.
